01 / Meaning

Hot, precise, dependable.

Prefire removes the tension between “the interface looks ready” and “we know it did not break”. The fire is the final warm-up before release, not visual noise.

01

Hot, not loud

Fire marks action and verification. It stays focused instead of taking over every surface.

02

Precise, not cold

Code, grids and status labels create clarity. Paper and light keep it human.

03

Fast, not fragile

Motion follows the path from preview to test. The language never promises magic.
