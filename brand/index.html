02

The logo. Flame and word.

The flame sits in a rounded square in the mark color. The word is Inter Semibold with tight tracking, set beside the mark at the same height.

Prefire
clear space: half the mark height on every side
  • MinimumLockup 20 px tall on screen, 7 mm in print. Mark alone 16 px.
  • Mark aloneApp icons, avatars, favicons, and where the name is already nearby.
  • NeverRecolor the flame, stretch the mark, add outlines or shadows to the word, or put it on a busy photo.

05

Graphic language. Rays, grain, real screens.

Four pieces build every surface. Use rays once per page, behind the main message, and let product shots do the rest.

Ember rays

Parallel bands at 36°, ember to rose, softly blurred and faded out at the edges. Behind heroes and closing calls only.

without grain
with grain

Grain

Fine noise over rays and gradients, and very faintly over the whole page. It keeps the fire from looking like a flat CSS fill.

Product shots

Real prefire render output in a dark phone frame with a warm glow underneath. Light and dark renders of the same screen sit side by side.
